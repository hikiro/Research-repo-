## Required structure for future research

Each substantial finding should include:
- claim or question;
- evidence label;
- source and publication details, or exact project artifact/test;
- context and scope;
- practical animation/rigging/production implication;
- counterexamples and limitations;
- validation method;
- date and links to related entries.
