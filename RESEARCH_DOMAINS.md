# Research Domains

## Program scope

Build a context-sensitive, queryable research corpus for animation and production. The program should connect language and action semantics with acting choices, body mechanics, staging, rigging, camera, sound, and measurable quality checks.

The project record specifies seven permanent domains, but only the first domain is currently recoverable with confidence. Do not invent names or scopes for the other six.

## AI-01 — Action Semantics and Character Intention

**Status:** APPROVED as the first permanent research domain.

**Purpose:** Understand how an action communicates what a character wants, chooses, attempts, feels, and causes in a particular context.

**Requested corpus dimensions:**
- verbs describing actions and motion;
- nouns describing actors, objects, body parts, props, and environmental elements;
- adjectives describing pose, motion quality, state, and appearance;
- adverbs describing manner, timing, intensity, and dynamics;
- sound descriptions associated with actions, materials, contact, and emotional intent.

**Context fields to preserve:**
- character identity, anatomy, morphology, and scale;
- action and intended outcome;
- emotional and motivational context;
- object properties and affordances;
- environment and spatial relationships;
- shot size, camera angle, framing, and screen direction;
- timing, rhythm, and relevant sound;
- reference evidence and confidence.

**Research questions:**
1. What visible cues make an action readable?
2. How does intention change the preparation, execution, follow-through, and reaction?
3. Which movement details are essential to distinguish similar verbs?
4. How do body plan, size, mass, and anatomy change the expression of the same intention?
5. How do object contact, resistance, balance, and consequence affect believability?
6. Which observations are supported by references, and which remain hypotheses?

**Evidence status:** The domain approval and requested corpus dimensions are recorded from prior project decisions. Individual animation principles and lexical mappings still require sourced examples and validation.
